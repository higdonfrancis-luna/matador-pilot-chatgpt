SEO is the backbone of every good online marketing campaign. Our SEO processes have been developed over the last 20 years, and include a mix of the following:

Why Matador

What makes Matador different
than every other marketing provider?

Intellectual property

Members retain 100% of the intellectual property generated by the MC for them. Unlike vendors like FindLaw, Justia, and Scorpion, you own your website, content, and all accounts (such as call tracking, call answering, etc.) created on your behalf.

Demonstrable Results

We compete in the most competitive markets in the country. Each month, we sit down with you to review the most important aspects of your marketing program: (1) rankings, (2) phone calls/new contacts, and (3) new cases.

TurnKey

Matador handles all of the work to get sites to move up in rankings. Members gain information about the latest changes in the search engine algorithms, but they are not required to personally do any of the work. All work is done in-house, in the United States.

Referrals

Because of the size of the Co-Op, we are able to facilitate referrals with different Member firms across the country.

Member discounts

It is important that each Member have access to the best ancillary services needed to operate your firm. This includes call tracking, call answering, intake software, review generation, and more. More often than not, we have spent time to vet a high-quality service and secured a discount on behalf of our Members.

unparalleled data

We are in a unique position to understand which keywords and methods actually produce revenue. Our keyword recommendations are a mix of spending time to understand your practice, weighed against the data we have amassed over the years.

No long term agreements

Unlike a traditional vendor, the Co-Op does not require, or offer, long-term agreements. While the SEO game often takes between 6 and 12 months to yield results, we engage in 60-day bilateral agreements. In other words, we make it worth your while every single month.

Not All Sites Are eligible

Unfortunately, not all sites can be accepted into the Co-Op. Sites must meet certain criteria to be considered. These include the strength of the site, the quality of the backlinks to the site, the size of the market, and more. If you are unsure if you are a fit, we urge you to get in touch regardless and will at least point you in the right direction.
